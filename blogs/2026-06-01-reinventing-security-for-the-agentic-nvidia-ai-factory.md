Palo Alto Networks announced the integration of Cortex XSIAM with NVIDIA's DOCA Argus framework, enabling real-time AI-powered security operations within NVIDIA AI factories. The solution leverages NVIDIA BlueField processors to detect kernel-level threats without installing host agents. Combined with Prisma AIRS, the platform delivers unified security across the entire AI lifecycle while maintaining performance through hardware-offloaded protections.
