Photo by Sebastian Svenson on Unsplash The PAN-OS Terraform provider v2.0.0 is now GA. Since the first major release of our Terraform provider v1.0.0 in 2018, there have been many changes to the Terraform ecosystem, underlying libraries, and practitioners’ use of Terraform to manage PAN-OS configuration. Most importantly, we have been observing the use cases our customers are trying to solve with the panos provider and its limitations.
