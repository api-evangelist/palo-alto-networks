Modern attackers using AI and evasion tactics are bypassing traditional network security defenses that rely on content inspection and outdated threat intelligence feeds. Organizations must shift their approach to monitor the IP layer directly and implement real-time, behavior-based threat detection rather than depending on static blocklists that lag behind rapidly rotating attacker infrastructure.
